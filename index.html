02 — SEO

The tool that reads Google before you write.

Surfer SEO scrapes the top 10 results for your target keyword, extracts word count, structure, NLP terms, and headings — then scores your draft in real time as you write. It's not an AI writer; it's an AI editor that knows what ranking looks like.

Why freelancers use it

  • First-pass outlines already optimized for the SERP
  • Real-time "Content Score" so you don't waste a revision cycle
  • Surfer AI can produce a complete first draft, then you rewrite it

Pricing reality

Essential plan is $89/mo (limited audits). Serious freelancers need Scale at $199/mo. There's no useful free tier — Surfer makes money by being the only tool that does this well.

Surfer SEO

03 — Audio + Video

The editor where deleting words cuts the audio.

Descript treats audio and video like a text document. Transcribe an hour-long podcast in 2 minutes. Delete filler words ("um", "uh") with one click. Edit by deleting text — the timeline re-renders. It's the only editor freelancers actually finish projects in.

Why it works

  • Auto-transcription in 23 languages
  • "Remove filler words" saves 5-10 minutes per 30-min recording
  • Overdub AI lets you fix a mis-spoken word by typing the correction
  • Screen recording + edit in the same tool (great for course creators)

Pricing reality

Free tier = 1 hour of transcription/mo (not useful). Hobbyist $24/mo = 10 hours. Pro $33/mo = 40 hours + Overdub.

04 — Voice

The voice generator that sounds like a person, not a model.

ElevenLabs is the only voice AI that's hard to clock on a phone call. It supports 29 languages, voice cloning from a 30-second sample, and an API that costs $0.30 per 1K characters. Freelancers use it for narration, ad voiceovers, podcast intros, and short-form video at scale.

Honest limitations

  • Long-form (>5 min) audio drifts in tone — best for sub-2-min clips
  • Voice cloning requires consent + commercial rights (don't clone people without permission)
  • Free tier gives you 10K characters/mo — enough to test, not enough for production

06 — Email

The email platform built for creators, not enterprises.

Kit (formerly ConvertKit) is the email service most freelance writers and creators use because it doesn't punish you for having under 1,000 subscribers. Free up to 10K subscribers. Automations are visual. Commerce (paid newsletters) is built in.

Why not Mailchimp?

Mailchimp charges you once you cross 500 subscribers, then upsells you into a "customer journey builder" you'll never use. Kit's pricing is linear, predictable, and the deliverability is comparable.

07 — Workspace

The doc tool that thinks alongside you.

Notion AI lives where you already work. You can ask it to summarize a long SOP, draft a meeting agenda, translate a doc, or generate action items from notes — without leaving the page. The $10/mo add-on is one of the cheapest AI upgrades you can buy.

Honest review

Notion AI is not the strongest foundation model. It's a convenient one. For serious writing, you still want Jasper or Claude. But for the 50 small tasks per day ("summarize this", "rewrite this shorter", "extract the action items"), it's the lowest-friction option.

Frequently asked, honestly answered.

Are these tools safe for client work?
All eight tools are SOC 2 or GDPR compliant and used by agencies billing $1M+/yr. None of them train on your inputs by default. For high-stakes client work, turn off training in the settings — Jasper, Surfer, Notion AI, and ElevenLabs all support this.
Will AI replace freelance marketers?
AI replaces the boring parts of the job — first drafts, transcription, meta descriptions, list pulls. The freelancer who ships a first draft in 20 minutes instead of 3 hours wins. Tools on this list are leverage, not replacement.
Which one is best for a solo freelancer on a budget?
Jasper + Surfer covers 80% of content marketing needs for $138/mo combined. Skip the rest until revenue from clients covers the cost. Writecream's free tier is enough for outreach if you only send <500 emails/mo.
What about ChatGPT or Claude directly?
For one-off tasks, raw ChatGPT/Claude is fine. But for repeatable client work, the tools on this list are better: Surfer reads the SERP, Jasper learns your brand voice once, Descript does podcast editing in 20 minutes instead of 6 hours.
